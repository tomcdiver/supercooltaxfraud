Propaganda Hall

A space for public ideas, visibility, and momentum.

Revolutionary Rutherford treats public messaging as a core part of political organizing. Messages should be clear, memorable, and hard to ignore.

Propaganda Hall is where the group frames its ideas, highlights its priorities, and turns discussion into public attention. It is designed to show that communication is not a side task — it is part of the campaign itself.

Highlights

Three principles of the hall.

Visibility

Make the message easy to see, easy to understand, and impossible to overlook.

Framing

Turn issues into language that explains the stakes and shapes the public conversation.

Momentum

Build recurring attention, repeat the key arguments, and keep the public engaged.

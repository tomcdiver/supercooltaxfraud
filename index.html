About the group

Ideas, organizing, and momentum.

Revolutionary Socialists of Rutherford is a Rutherford College group built around bold ideas, open discussion, and a public-facing commitment to making political engagement more active and more visible.

The group brings together leadership, planning, and communication in a way designed to keep the message clear, the mission coherent, and the future open to revision as circumstances change.

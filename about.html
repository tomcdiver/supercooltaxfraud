About

What the group is trying to build.

Revolutionary Rutherford is a Rutherford College political group built around public engagement, strategic planning, and a willingness to challenge the ordinary.

The group aims to make activism and discussion more visible, more intentional, and more connected to the people who participate in campus life. The goal is not just to speak loudly, but to speak clearly and consistently.

The approach blends political ambition with practical coordination: leadership sets direction, planning keeps ideas moving, and public visibility ensures the message reaches beyond the immediate room.

Core values

How the group operates.

Visibility

Make the group easy to understand, easy to find, and impossible to ignore.

Strategy

Use planning and communication to translate ideas into action.

Momentum

Keep the public conversation alive, active, and connected to the mission.

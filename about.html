About

What the group is trying to build.

Revolutionary Socialists of Rutherford is a Rutherford College political group built around public engagement, strategic planning, and a willingness to challenge the ordinary.

The group aims to make activism and discussion more visible, more intentional, and more connected to the people who participate in campus life. The goal is not just to speak loudly, but to organize clearly and act with purpose.

The approach blends political ambition with practical coordination: leadership sets direction, planning keeps ideas moving, and public visibility ensures the message reaches beyond the room it starts in.

Core values

How the group operates.

Visibility

Make the group easy to understand, easy to find, and impossible to ignore.

Strategy

Combine ambition with planning so the message has structure and momentum.

Participation

Encourage open engagement and a wider culture of discussion around political ideas.

Adaptability

Roles and responsibilities may shift as the organization grows and evolves.
